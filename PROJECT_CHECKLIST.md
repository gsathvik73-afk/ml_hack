## Current protocol: disclose the 80-to-78 change

- The supplied protocol specifies **80 training trips**, 10 student-test trips and 10 final evaluator trips.
- The user's current training instruction excludes `00000033` and `00000054`; [the exclusion file](outputs/training_exclusions.json) records that choice. The raw files and earlier all-80 results remain available.
- New shared runs therefore use **78 training trips** and preserve the existing assignments in [group_folds.csv](outputs/folds/group_folds.csv). Do not quietly regenerate folds or describe the remaining folds as newly balanced GroupKFold folds.
- This is a **documented deviation from the stated 80-trip protocol**, not automatic compliance. Explain the reason, show the retained 80-trip baseline, and resolve whether the submission must include an all-80 final experiment. Do not erase or relabel the earlier results.
- The active schema is **`direction_32`**, with the ordered predictor list in [feature_columns.json](results/direction_features/feature_columns.json). The older [fold/metric contract](outputs/folds/contract.json) still lists the historical 24 predictors; use it for folds and metric rules, and the registry's feature-list path for current model inputs.
- Candidate generation remains frozen at **210 m, K=100**. Training-derived reference-label flagging remains **208.357058 m**, with alignment costs capped at **150 m**. Labels are ordered reference-route DP associations, not independently verified GPS-row ground truth.

The canonical model integration procedure is in [docs/MODEL_INTEGRATION.md](docs/MODEL_INTEGRATION.md). [models/registry.json](models/registry.json) is the current machine-readable inventory; an entry alone is not proof that all of its files have been produced and validated.

## Consolidated report outline

Write the final report from actual outputs after the missing model evidence is available. Each claim and table should name its producing notebook and saved result file.

1. **Problem, dataset and partitions.** Explain file formats, projection, the 80/10/10 supplied protocol, active 78-trip exclusion decision and the unchanged final evaluator boundary. Include missing-value/time-gap/geographical/connectivity findings and representative maps.
2. **Labels, candidates and features.** Explain ordered DP labels, uncertainty, cost cap and flags; frozen radius/K and candidate miss rate; all 32 predictors and why they were added; fold-only imputation/scaling; future GPS context and its whole-trip inference assumption.
3. **Five-model comparison.** One table using the common point-level and candidate-row definitions, CV mean/SD, pooled OOF, student-test metrics, continuity and clearly scoped timings. Include all five algorithms, not five XGBoost variants.
4. **A subsection for every experimented model.** Why the model is suitable; strengths and limitations; initial and selected hyperparameters; training objective; preprocessing and sampling scope; actual CV/student-test results; evidence for over/underfitting. Document original incompatible DT experiments separately from the canonical refit.
5. **Required plots.** [Current-model visualization code](14_model_visualizations.ipynb) already produces actual-result plots for available trained models; rerun after all five are ready. Cross-model CV Macro F1; student-test Top-1 and Macro F1; log-loss comparison; relevant confusion matrices; actual optimization history. Use epoch-wise loss curves only if a model genuinely produces them. Do not draw artificial training histories for KNN, trees or other non-iterative fits.
6. **Bayesian optimization.** Search distributions, objective, query sampling, completed trial count, best configuration, cost and tuned-versus-untuned results on matching scopes. Distinguish the historical 80/24 study from current 78/32 reuse.
7. **HMM/Viterbi and sequence results.** Hidden states, emissions, transition formula and limitations, gap/reset policy, parameter-selection data, posterior versus joint-path outputs, classifier-only versus decoded metrics, and cases where decoding reduced point accuracy.
8. **Geographical results.** Actual GPS, roads, reference and prediction layers. Include a representative screenshot in the PDF and link the interactive HTML/code in the repository.
9. **System and deployment.** Current model registry/export, API contract, Claude's UI handoff, dependency/run commands, two-laptop LAN flow, receiving-server logs and successful API/UI screenshots. State what inference timing includes.
10. **Conclusions, limitations and reproducibility.** Evidence-backed final choice after five-model comparison; unresolved protocol deviations; reference-label uncertainty; student-test reuse as development evaluation; final evaluator data unused; seed/version/configuration paths and AI-assistance disclosure as applicable.
